Rainbow Tilt

  • Tap a ball to start it rolling. Anywhere in its glowing beam above it works too, and you can use more than one finger.
  • Every flume is a different length, so every ball takes a different time to reach the square.
  • The square is a seesaw. As soon as any ball rolls past its middle, it rocks up and shuts out every ball that isn't already on it.
  • Time your starts so all seven balls get onto the square before it tips.
  • Drag to look around. Pinch to zoom.

Levels

Each level gives you a tighter window. From level 9, balls speed up on steep drops.
